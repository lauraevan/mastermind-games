About

A practice platform built on how learning actually works

Mastermind Academy is a free, standards-aligned practice platform for students in kindergarten through grade 12. We do not promise shortcuts. We build the two things research keeps confirming matter most: deliberate, spaced practice and honest feedback.

Deliberate practice

Every skill is broken into focused problems with immediate, specific feedback — the conditions under which people actually improve.

Adaptive difficulty

Problems scale to your grade and adjust as you work, keeping you in the productive zone between boredom and overwhelm.

Honest measurement

Mastery, accuracy, streaks and full assessments show you where you truly stand — not where you hope you stand.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mastermind Academy free?

Yes. Create an account and use every subject, drill and assessment at no cost.

What grades and subjects are covered?

Kindergarten through grade 12, across mathematics, algebra, geometry, calculus, reading and language, Spanish, science, history and geography.

How does adaptive difficulty work?

You set your grade level, which sets the baseline. As you answer, the system nudges difficulty up after a run of correct answers and eases it back when you struggle, so the work stays challenging but achievable.

Where is my progress stored?

Entirely in your browser, on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Clearing your browser data will reset your account.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The platform runs in any modern browser with no downloads or plugins.

What are assessments?

Full-length exams of 15, 30 or 45 questions drawn from a subject and grade. You answer at your own pace, then receive a score report with every solution explained.
